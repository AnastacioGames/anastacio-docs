<p class="mag-kicker">3D View</p>
<h2 class="mag-title">The scene is where everything happens</h2>
<p class="mag-lede">The 3D View shows your game's world. You move the camera, pick objects, transform, snap and check the scene from different angles. Almost everything starts here, and almost everything can be done with the mouse and the keyboard.</p>
<div class="mag-flow"><span>View</span><b>→</b><span>Select</span><b>→</b><span>Transform</span></div>

<section class="mag-feature">
<div class="mag-num">01</div>
<div>
<p class="mag-tag">Navigation</p>
<h3 class="mag-h">Orbit, Pan and Zoom</h3>
<p class="mag-big">Rotate, move and zoom the camera without leaving the scene.</p>
<p>In the <b>View</b> menu, the <b>Navigation</b> section gathers the camera movements. You can orbit around the object, drag the view sideways, zoom in or out and even tilt the camera with <b>Roll Left</b> and <b>Roll Right</b>.</p>
<ul class="mag-list">
<li><strong>Zoom Border</strong> zooms into just the area you draw.</li>
<li><strong>Zoom Camera 1:1</strong> shows the scene exactly as the active camera frames it.</li>
<li><strong>Fly</strong> and <b>Walk</b> move through the scene like in a game, with the keyboard.</li>
</ul>
</div>
{/* pending image: View > Navigation menu open */}
</section>

{/* pending image: top bar with the View, Add, Object, Transform and Snap menus */}

<div class="mag-grid">
<article class="mag-card">
<p class="mag-tag">Transform</p>
<h3 class="mag-h">Transform</h3>
<p>Moves, rotates and scales the selection. The menu also brings deformations such as <b>To Sphere</b>, <b>Shear</b>, <b>Bend</b> and <b>Push/Pull</b>.</p>
</article>
<article class="mag-card">
<p class="mag-tag">Snapping</p>
<h3 class="mag-h">Snap</h3>
<p>Moves the selection to the grid, to the cursor or to the active object. It can also move the 3D cursor to the selection, the center or the grid.</p>
</article>
<article class="mag-card">
<p class="mag-tag">Views</p>
<h3 class="mag-h">Viewpoint</h3>
<p>Shows the scene from above, below, front, back, right, left or through the camera. With <b>Cameras</b>, you can use any camera object as the view.</p>
</article>
</div>

<blockquote class="mag-quote">“Pick the right view before you move. A bad angle hides the mistake.”</blockquote>

{/* pending image: Snap menu open over the scene */}

<section class="mag-classics">
<h3 class="mag-section">Also in this window</h3>
<div class="mag-cols">
<p><strong>Show/Hide.</strong> Shows hidden objects, hides the selection or hides everything that is not selected.</p>
<p><strong>Proportional Editing.</strong> Also moves the vertices around the selection, with a smooth falloff. You choose the falloff type in the same menu.</p>
<p><strong>UV Mapping.</strong> Gathers the UV projections, such as Smart Project, Cube, Cylinder and Sphere, and Reset.</p>
<p><strong>Mirror.</strong> Mirrors the selection across an axis, with options to clear and to keep the positions.</p>
</div>
